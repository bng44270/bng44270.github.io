ServiceNow Exit Strategy

As someone who began their career in the IT Infrastructure and DevOps spaces I had the opportunity to use a variety of hardware and software and, because I was the way I was, I took every opportunity to write scripts to make my life a little easier and, let's be honest, a little more interesting. It was this love for writing scripts that eventually led me to an opportunity to become a ServiceNow developer. For the past 8 years I've dove deep and built my development skills on the ServiceNow platform. While I have loved my experience, I have been troubled by the number of people I see enter the SerivceNow space right out of college. This troubles me because of the relatively limited and propietary skill set they are gaining and that the rest of the non-ServiceNow IT world does not know how to properly recognize the skills held by people who have only ServiceNow experience.
